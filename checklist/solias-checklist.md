# SOLIAS Checklist

The complete SOLIAS checklist contains 20 items organized into six reporting functions, with concise responses expected for each item. Authors may provide page references when the relevant information already appears in the manuscript and may designate an item as not applicable when the justification is stated.

| No. | Reporting function | Item | Reporting question | Minimum useful response |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Purpose and population | Social problem | What need, inequity, risk, or service problem motivates the work? | A specific problem and the setting in which it occurs. |
| 2 | Purpose and population | Target population | Who is expected to experience the intended benefit? | Population characteristics relevant to use and evaluation. |
| 3 | Purpose and population | Excluded or at-risk groups | Who may be underserved, excluded, burdened, or harmed? | Relevant groups and plausible mechanisms of exclusion, burden, or harm. |
| 4 | Purpose and population | Intended outcome | What longer-term outcome motivates the research program? | A named user, operational, or social outcome. |
| 5 | Purpose and population | Stakeholders | Who uses, governs, maintains, or is affected by the system? | Users, practitioners, institutions, maintainers, and affected non-users. |
| 6 | System and pathway | NLP function | What language capability does the system provide? | The task, input, output, and role of the language component. |
| 7 | System and pathway | Use context | Where, by whom, and under what constraints is use anticipated? | Setting, user role, workflow, available resources, and relevant constraints. |
| 8 | System and pathway | Theory of change | How is the NLP function expected to contribute to the intended outcome? | Intermediate user actions, institutional responses, assumptions, and dependencies. |
| 9 | Evidence and alignment | Demonstrated evidence level | What is the highest outcome directly evaluated? | SOLIAS level, measure, population, context, and evaluation setting. |
| 10 | Evidence and alignment | Claimed outcome level and gap | What is the highest outcome presented as a result, and which transitions remain unevaluated? | The (E, C, G) profile and a page reference to the relevant claim. |
| 11 | Evidence and alignment | Evaluation measures | Why do the selected measures represent the outcome being evaluated? | Measures, timing, comparison conditions, and interpretation. |
| 12 | Outcome evaluation | Output quality | Are the output properties relevant to the intended use evaluated? | Criteria such as factuality, clarity, uncertainty expression, usefulness, or harmfulness. |
| 13 | Outcome evaluation | User outcome | Are task success, comprehension, usability, accessibility, or error recovery evaluated? | Population, task, study setting, comparison, and outcome measure. |
| 14 | Outcome evaluation | Decision or behavior | Are meaningful decisions or actions evaluated? | Actor, decision or action, comparison condition, and measured effect. |
| 15 | Outcome evaluation | Operational outcome | Are service, workflow, uptake, workload, or institutional outcomes evaluated? | Operational setting, process, comparison, timeframe, and outcome measure. |
| 16 | Outcome evaluation | Social outcome | Are population or community outcomes evaluated with an appropriate design? | Population, setting, study design, outcome, timeframe, and causal assumptions. |
| 17 | Responsible operation | Equity and accessibility | Are distributional effects and barriers involving language, literacy, disability, geography, devices, or connectivity assessed? | Relevant groups and barriers, distributional estimates where applicable, and evaluation or mitigation measures. |
| 18 | Responsible operation | Safety and failure-to-harm | How could system failures affect people or institutions, and what safeguards are required? | Failure mode, exposure pathway, consequence, detection, escalation, and mitigation. |
| 19 | Responsible operation | Maintenance and monitoring | What changes after release could affect performance or outcomes? | Monitoring responsibilities, drift, policy or service changes, update criteria, and retirement conditions. |
| 20 | Calibrated conclusion | Evidence-calibrated conclusion | What has been demonstrated, for whom and where, and what remains prospective? | Separate statements of the observed result, broader intended outcome, and next evaluation target. |

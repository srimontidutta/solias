# Venue Adoption

The paper proposes beginning with usability and agreement testing before stronger venue requirements are considered. A short-form SOLIAS request can be incorporated into submission or review materials while keeping the reporting burden compact.

## Suggested short-form request

Authors whose work connects an NLP contribution to outcomes for people, institutions, or communities may provide the following SOLIAS fields:

1. **Demonstrated evidence level (E):** highest outcome layer directly evaluated, with the relevant population and context.
2. **Claimed outcome level (C):** highest outcome presented as a result.
3. **Claim-evidence gap (G):** `max(0, C - E)`.
4. **Theory of change:** concise account of how the NLP function is expected to contribute to the broader intended outcome.
5. **Evidence-calibrated conclusion:** statement of the demonstrated outcome for the evaluated population and context, with broader intended outcomes identified as prospective where appropriate.

## Copy-ready form

```text
SOLIAS short form

Population and context:

E - demonstrated evidence level:
C - claimed outcome level:
G = max(0, C - E):

Theory of change:

Evidence-calibrated conclusion:
```

A venue may request the complete 20-item author form when the additional population, outcome-evaluation, responsible-operation, and maintenance fields are useful for review.

## Review use

Reviewers can use E to identify the object of methodological appraisal and C and G to examine the relationship between the conclusion and the evidence. A bounded contribution can therefore be reviewed on the methodological quality of the outcome it evaluates while broader intended outcomes remain part of the research program.

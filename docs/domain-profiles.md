# Domain Profiles

SOLIAS uses a domain-general outcome ontology so that studies in different application areas can share a common reporting language while domain experts retain responsibility for selecting appropriate outcomes, measures, comparisons, safeguards, and methods.

A domain profile may add:

- examples suited to the application setting;
- recommended outcome measures or measurement considerations;
- safeguards and responsible-operation guidance;
- implementation guidance for authors, reviewers, or institutions; and
- domain-specific interpretation notes.

Profiles preserve the meanings of the six evidence levels and the `(E, C, G)` representation. This arrangement supports domain-specific methodological guidance while retaining a common classification across application areas.

## Proposing a profile

Proposals can be submitted through the **Domain profile proposal** issue form and should identify the setting, the additional guidance, the stakeholders consulted or affected, and the way the profile preserves the core SOLIAS classification. For substantive revisions, the governance record includes the supporting evidence, stakeholder feedback, final decision, and effective version.
